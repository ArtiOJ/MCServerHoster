Server Manager for iPhone: building and installing
===================================================

This folder is an Xcode project made by Godot. It needs a Mac with Xcode 15 or newer
and an Apple Developer account.

1. Open ServerManager.xcodeproj in Xcode.
2. Click the ServerManager project in the left column, pick the ServerManager target,
   open "Signing & Capabilities":
   - Team: choose your team (the project comes with a placeholder, CHANGEME00).
   - Bundle Identifier: io.github.artioj.servermanager. If Xcode says it is taken,
     change it to something unique, e.g. com.<yourname>.servermanager.
3. Put it on an iPhone to try it:
   - Connect the iPhone with a cable and trust the Mac.
   - On the iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode > On (it restarts).
   - In Xcode pick the iPhone at the top, then Product > Run.
4. Share it through TestFlight (no cable needed for the testers):
   - In App Store Connect (appstoreconnect.apple.com) create a new app with the same
     bundle identifier.
   - In Xcode pick "Any iOS Device (arm64)", then Product > Archive.
   - In the Organizer window: Distribute App > TestFlight & App Store > Upload.
   - When the build shows up in App Store Connect under TestFlight, add testers by
     email. They install the TestFlight app and accept the invite.

When the app first connects to a computer on the same Wi-Fi, iPhone asks to allow
"local network" access. Allow it, or computers at home can't be reached.
